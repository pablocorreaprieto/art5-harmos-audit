## Declarations

**Data availability.** The prompts, call plans, raw model outputs with their SHA-256 hashes, analysis code, results, the records of the AI review sessions and the scripts that check every number of the article are available at https://github.com/pablocorreaprieto/art5-harmos-audit, made public at submission (archived with a DOI on acceptance). An anonymised copy of the repository is ready for the reviewers and can be sent on request.

**Ethics approval and consent.** The study involved no human participants and no personal data: its data are texts generated by language models through their public APIs. No ethical approval was required, and consent to participate or to publish does not apply.

**Competing interests.** The author holds a personal paid subscription to Anthropic's Claude service and otherwise has no employment, consulting, shareholding or other financial relationship with Anthropic, OpenAI or Mistral AI. The Anthropic calls were paid with promotional credits that Anthropic granted to the author's API account (see Funding). Anthropic made two of the audited models and the AI assistant used to carry out the study (see Use of generative AI) but had no other role in the study.

**Funding.** This research received no specific grant from any funding agency. The API calls cost 41.12 USD for the main study and 2.26 USD for the pilot. The Anthropic calls (25.44 USD in the main study) were paid with promotional credits that Anthropic granted to the author's API account. Before the main run that account held 206.87 USD of credit, of which 201.87 USD were promotional, and the purchased credit was not used. The OpenAI and Mistral calls were paid by the author.

**Use of generative AI.** The study was carried out with an AI assistant, Claude Opus 5.5 (claude-opus-5-5, Anthropic), under a written protocol whose rules the author set. The assistant searched the literature, designed the study in detail, wrote the preregistration and the code, ran the data collection and the analyses, and drafted the manuscript, including the interpretation of the results, except the advice to teachers in the Implications, which the author wrote. Separate sessions of Claude models (claude-opus-5-5, claude-sonnet-5-5 and claude-fable-5-1), one of which is also an audited model, reviewed the design, the code and the text and checked each citation against its source. A later session (claude-fable-5-2) read the manuscript before submission and reworded the Method paragraph on the use of an AI assistant and one sentence of the Competing interests. The author chose the topic, took design decisions (among them the number of providers and the replacement of a model due to be retired), approved the design before data collection and the manuscript before submission, and takes full responsibility for the work. All prompts, outputs and review records are in the repository.

**Author contributions (CRediT).** The author: conceptualization, funding acquisition, methodology, project administration, resources, supervision, writing – original draft (the advice to teachers in the Implications), writing – review and editing. The tasks carried out by the AI assistant, which is not an author, are described under Use of generative AI.
