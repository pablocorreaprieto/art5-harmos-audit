To the Editors of the *British Journal of Educational Technology*

Dear Editors,

Please find attached the manuscript "{{ms_title}}", which I submit for consideration as an original research article.

Teachers are advised to name their pupils' grade when they ask a language model for a reading text, but grade labels are local: the labels of French-speaking Switzerland (« 5H », « 5P ») name pupils two years younger than the fifth year of primary school in France. In a preregistered audit of {{n_texts}} French reading texts written by {{n_models_word}} large language models, the bare labels gave texts close to the lexical demand of the matching age on average, but the models departed from their own age-only texts in both directions, one lighter model by about two years, and the model that wrote a text mattered more for its vocabulary than the wording of the grade. The article gives teachers and tool developers a simple rule, state the age and read the text, and shows why evaluations of AI tools for teachers should report results model by model.

The study follows a recommendation published in this journal, that studies of language models in education report the exact models, prompts and settings they used (Yan et al., 2024): every prompt, parameter and raw output is archived, and the results are reported for each model as well as pooled. It involved no human participants; its data are texts generated through the providers' APIs. The hypotheses, measure, exclusion rules, sample size and analyses were preregistered before data collection. The deposited preregistration contains the address of the study repository, which names me; for that reason I give the link to the preregistration on the title page for the editor only, and reviewers receive an anonymised copy of the repository that includes the preregistration text.

The study was carried out with an AI assistant under a written protocol, and the manuscript declares in full what the assistant did, which separate AI sessions reviewed the work, and how the Anthropic API calls were paid (mostly with promotional credits from Anthropic, which also made two of the audited models and the assistant). The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere and has not been published.
